# Tutor onboarding pilot

Status: prepared; not run with real participants. No production environment has been provided.

## People and preparation

Assign a pilot owner, qualified subject reviewers, an identity/qualification reviewer, five teachers covering Windows and macOS, and two student participants. Include school and university teaching, multiple teaching languages, and one unlisted subject. Use account references in these notes rather than identity documents or document numbers.

Complete a dry run with synthetic evidence first. Before collecting participant evidence, configure the private storage key, file retention, reviewer access and the organisation’s verification process. Reviewers must inspect the actual evidence and use appropriate authoritative sources; the application records the result and does not perform an automated identity or background check.

## Teacher exercise

1. Create a teacher account and complete the profile without facilitator help.
2. Select multiple categories, subjects, academic stages and teaching languages. Confirm that each teaching area describes the intended offering.
3. Upload or link an introduction video.
4. Upload the required identity evidence and support for any claimed qualifications through the private verification area.
5. Complete the approved tasks for the selected academic levels. Confirm that an unlisted subject uses manual suitability review.
6. Submit, receive the in-app update and email, and locate the review status.
7. Respond to a change request, replace an incorrect document, and resubmit.
8. After approval, find the public profile and check that private documents and review notes are absent.
9. Remove required evidence and confirm that the listing requires a new review.

Record the time taken, steps requiring help, unclear language, failed uploads, missing feedback, and any task that does not fit the selected academic stage. Aim for unassisted completion; record delays for video preparation and gathering documents separately.

## Reviewer exercise

1. Inspect category/academic-level tasks and their review criteria. Record qualified expertise and content findings before approving the current content version.
2. Download private evidence, verify its source and authenticity using the organisation’s process, and record concise findings without copying identity numbers.
3. Watch the introduction video. Assess each task independently against the criteria and record the reasons for the outcome.
4. Request a concrete correction, inspect the resubmission and record the final decision.
5. Check that changed tasks require a fresh content approval and updated teacher response.

## Student exercise

Find the teacher using category, academic stage and teaching-language filters. Open the intended class, enroll in the pilot’s free class, and join after the teacher starts it. Confirm that teacher whiteboard updates appear. Paid checkout is outside this acceptance flow.
