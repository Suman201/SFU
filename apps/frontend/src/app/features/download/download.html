Choose the right installer

On a Mac

Open the Apple menu, then About This Mac. Choose Apple silicon for an Apple M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel processor. Open the downloaded DMG and drag Sapiovian Classroom into Applications.

On Windows

Open Settings → System → About and check System type. Most PCs use Intel / AMD (x64). Choose ARM64 for an ARM-based PC. Run the installer and follow the setup steps.

Stay ready for class

Sign in with your existing student or teacher account. Allow camera and microphone access when needed, then check your devices before joining. App updates are available in Settings and wait until you leave class.
